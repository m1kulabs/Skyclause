## Participation and rules

Registration is open. Teams have 2–5 members. Judging criteria will be released soon.

### Eligibility

Only Asia Pacific University (APU) students can take part. Every participant signs in with their own account and adds their details before joining a team. Contact the organisers on Discord if you need clarification before registering.

### Team formation

Teams must have 2–5 members, including the leader. Team members must be from the same university. The leader creates a team in the workspace and shares a private invite link; every member signs in with their own account. Ask in Discord if you are looking for teammates.

### Submissions

Prepare a project title, challenge, summary of at least 50 characters, HTTPS repository link, and PDF pitch deck up to 10 MB. A demo link is optional. Save drafts in the team workspace, then review and confirm your final entry.

### Judging

Judging criteria will be released soon. Assessment is scheduled for 11–14 November, followed by the top-five announcement on 15 November. Follow the community channels for the criteria.

## Next steps

Registration and the team workspace are separate. Complete them in order.

1.  **Register for the event (required):** Sign in to the [team workspace](team-workspace.md) with your own account and add your participant details.
2.  **Set up your team (after registering):** Sign in, create or join a team, and save your project draft. Leaders invite members and manage the final entry.
3.  **Stay connected (optional):** Join [Discord](https://discord.gg/uTMW3YXV3b) for questions and team coordination. Follow the social channels for event updates.

## FAQ

Still have a question? [Ask the organisers on Discord](https://discord.gg/uTMW3YXV3b).

### Who is this event for?

Only Asia Pacific University (APU) students can take part. This hackathon is organised by APU School of Technology, with GDGOC APU supporting event coordination and execution. If you are unsure whether you can join, contact the GDGOC APU team on Discord before registering.

### Can I join without a team?

Teams must have 2–5 members, including the leader. Team members must be from the same university. If you are looking for teammates, ask in the Discord community. In the workspace, the leader creates the team and invites each member to join with their own account.

### Do I need AI experience?

The schedule includes an online problem statement briefing and Gemini training on 3 November 2026, from 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM Malaysia time (MYT). Check the registration form or ask the organisers about any prerequisites before joining.

### Is creating a workspace account the same as registering?

Yes. Sign in to the team workspace with your own account and add your participant details to register. Then create or join a team and prepare your project. Use the same account each time you return.

### What do I need to submit?

The workspace requires a project title, a selected challenge, a summary of at least 50 characters, an HTTPS repository link, and a PDF pitch deck up to 10 MB. A demo link is optional. Only the team leader can edit and submit the final entry; final submission locks the project and PDF.

### How will projects be judged?

Judging criteria will be released soon. Assessment is scheduled for 11–14 November, with the top-five announcement on 15 November. Follow the community channels for the published criteria.

### When do registration and submissions close?

Registration closes on 8 November 2026 at 11:59 PM Malaysia time (MYT). The published prototype submission deadline is 10 November 2026 at 11:59 PM MYT. The workspace shows the submission window currently enforced by the organisers.
