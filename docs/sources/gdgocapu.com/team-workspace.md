## Signing in

Continue with Google or email. New accounts are created on first sign-in.

With email, the workspace sends a secure sign-in link. Open it in the same browser on the same device. No password is needed. Use your own email, not a shared team account.

## Registration steps

1.  **Register:** Sign in and add your participant details.
2.  **Workspace:** Sign in again to create a team and submit.

## Account safety

Never share your sign-in link or password in a public channel. For help, [ask the organisers on Discord](https://discord.gg/uTMW3YXV3b).
