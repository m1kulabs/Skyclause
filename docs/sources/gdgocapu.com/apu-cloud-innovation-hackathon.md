Captured from the [hackathon home page](https://apu-cloud-innovation-hackathon.gdgocapu.com/) on 3 November 2026. ## Event overview

- **When:** 26 October – 17 November 2026
- **Where:** Asia Pacific University, Kuala Lumpur
- **Who:** Only APU students can take part.
- **Organised by:** APU School of Technology, supported by GDGOC APU
- **How to register:** Register your team in the [team workspace](team-workspace.md).

## Theme

When Cloud, IoT, and Network converge: bring the systems together and build an idea that connects them.

- **Cloud:** Compute beyond limits.
- **IoT:** Sense the world around you.
- **Network:** Connect it all.

## Challenge tracks

Pick a challenge that fits your idea. Teams can explore any track. The ideas listed under each track are ideas to explore, not required features.

### Smart Campus & Student Experience

Build connected experiences that make campus life more useful, accessible, and engaging.

- Finding and accessing campus services
- More accessible student experiences
- Connecting students with campus resources

### Intelligent Automation & Digital Transformation

Bring AI into everyday workflows and turn complex tasks into thoughtful digital solutions.

- Automating repetitive campus workflows
- Connecting devices with useful actions
- Making university processes easier to use

### Data-Driven Research & Intelligent Decision Making

Connect data with real questions and help people make better decisions.

- Turning research data into useful insights
- Helping people explore patterns in data
- Supporting clearer, evidence-based decisions

| 5th   | RM150   |

## Why take part

Come curious, leave capable: build something of your own, with a community around you.

- Explore an idea beyond the classroom.
- Connect with the APU developer community.
- Create a project you can share and keep improving.
