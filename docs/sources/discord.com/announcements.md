## Registration and updates

Posted 26 October 2026.

Organised by APU School of Technology, with event coordination and execution supported by GDGoC APU. Registration is open only to current APU students. Students from other institutions are not eligible. Form a team of **2–5 current APU students**, select one challenge, and use the official team workspace. Each participant signs in with their own account; the leader creates the team and shares its private invite link. Registration is free.

- **Team registration:** https://apu-cloud-innovation-hackathon.gdgocapu.com/
- **Key dates and briefing:** [#key-dates-and-briefing](key-dates-and-briefing.md)
- **Problem statements:** [#problem-statements](problem-statements.md)
- **Questions:** [#help-desk](help-desk.md)

The briefing, prototype deadline, and top-five dates are in the key dates channel above. The submission checklist is in [#submission-guidelines](submission-guidelines.md). We will post the Bevy joining link here when the event page is available. You can also follow [GDGoC APU on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/gdgoc.apu/) for event news.

## Eligibility update

Posted 30 October 2026.

The APU Cloud Innovation Hackathon 2026 is open **only to current APU students**. Students from other universities or colleges are not eligible to participate. Every member of a team must be a current APU student; teams should have 2–5 members.

Please use the [official registration website](https://apu-cloud-innovation-hackathon.gdgocapu.com/).

If you have already registered and are unsure whether your team meets this requirement, please contact the organizers through [#help-desk](help-desk.md). We apologise for the earlier wording that suggested the event was open to students from other institutions.

## Team formation reminder

Posted 2 November 2026.

If you have not formed a team yet, please do so now. This hackathon is strictly team-based: solo participation and solo submissions are not accepted. Every team must have 2–5 current APU students.

Looking for teammates? Post your APU programme, skills, and preferred challenge area in [#team-formation](team-formation.md), or reply to someone already looking there. Once your team is ready, the team leader should create it at https://apu-cloud-innovation-hackathon.gdgocapu.com/ and share the private invite link with the other members. Each member must sign in with their own account.

If you need help finding a team, ask in [#help-desk](help-desk.md). Please do not share phone numbers or email addresses publicly.
