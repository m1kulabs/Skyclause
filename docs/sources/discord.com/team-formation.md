## Finding your hackathon team

Teams need **2–5 current APU students** and every member must be enrolled at APU. If you are looking for teammates, introduce yourself with:

- Your APU programme and field of study
- Skills you can contribute
- Problem statement you are interested in
- Roles or skills your team is looking for

Reply in the channel or use Discord DMs to connect. Please do not post phone numbers, email addresses, or another person's personal details publicly. Once your team is ready, have **one team leader** create the team on the [official website](https://apu-cloud-innovation-hackathon.gdgocapu.com/) and share its private invite link. Each member signs in with their own account.

## Team matching check-in

Looking for a team? Reply with your APU programme, the skills you can contribute, and the challenge area that interests you.

Already have a team with an open spot? Reply with your APU programme, the challenge area you are exploring, and the skills you hope to find. Teams must have 2–5 current APU students. Students from other institutions are not eligible.

Please reply in the channel or move to DMs to coordinate. Do not share phone numbers, email addresses, or resumes in this public channel.
