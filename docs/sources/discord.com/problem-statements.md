The three challenge areas from the `#problem-statements` channel of the hackathon Discord, posted on 26 October 2026 and captured on 3 November 2026. Each team chooses one.

## Smart Campus & Student Experience

Improve student life, access to campus services, and connectivity. Example ideas: campus navigation, personalized onboarding, or an AI learning companion.

## Intelligent Automation & Digital Transformation

Use AI to streamline repetitive work and university workflows. Example ideas: HR/IT support, document processing, or student performance insights for lecturers.

## Data-Driven Research & Intelligent Decision Making

Turn data into useful insights for research and planning. Example ideas: research visualization, trend analysis, or resource forecasting.

## Choosing a problem statement

These are broad challenge areas; the example ideas are inspiration, not required solutions. Select **one** statement in the team registration form. The briefing will cover further guidance and questions.
