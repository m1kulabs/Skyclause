## About the hackathon

Organised by APU School of Technology, with event coordination and execution supported by GDGoC APU, this online hackathon is open to current **APU students only**. Form a team of **2–5 current APU students**, enter your APU details accurately on the registration website, choose one of the three problem statements, and build a solution using Google AI tools. Registration is free.

## Start here

1.  Read [#key-dates-and-briefing](key-dates-and-briefing.md) and [#problem-statements](problem-statements.md).
2.  Each student signs in; the team leader creates the team and shares its private invite link on the [registration website](https://apu-cloud-innovation-hackathon.gdgocapu.com/).
3.  Need teammates? Use [#team-formation](team-formation.md). Questions? Use [#help-desk](help-desk.md).

Check [#announcements](announcements.md) for official updates and follow [GDGoC APU on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/gdgoc.apu/).

## Community rules

Please be respectful, keep posts relevant, and do not share another person's contact details or files without their permission.
