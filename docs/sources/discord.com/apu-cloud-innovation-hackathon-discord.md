## Server description

Official Discord for APU Cloud Innovation Hackathon 2026, organised by APU School of Technology with event coordination and execution supported by GDGoC APU. Open only to current APU students. Find teammates, explore the three challenge areas, and follow official event updates.

## Captured channels

Channels are grouped by their category on the server. The `#event-updates`, `#results-and-winners`, and `#off-topic` channels had no posts at capture time. The `#landing`, `#introductions`, and `#qna` channels had only join notices, greetings, or a help request with no details, so they have no doc.

## Source discrepancies

The Discord posts and the [website](/docs/sources/gdgocapu.com/event-details.md) disagree on some details as of 3 November 2026. Each doc records its own source verbatim.

- **Briefing end time:** 8:00 PM in [#key-dates-and-briefing](key-dates-and-briefing.md) and [#announcements](announcements.md); 9:00 PM in [#faq-and-guidelines](faq-and-guidelines.md) and on the website.
- **Demo link:** optional in [#faq-and-guidelines](faq-and-guidelines.md) and on the website; listed without "optional" in [#submission-guidelines](submission-guidelines.md).
