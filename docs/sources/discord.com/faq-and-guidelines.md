## Joining and registration

### Who can join?

Current APU students only. Students from other universities or colleges are not eligible to participate.

### How many people can be on a team?

Two to five, including the team leader. Every team member must be a current APU student. Please enter your APU details accurately when registering.

### Can I join on my own?

Registration is for teams of two to five. If you need teammates, post in [#team-formation](team-formation.md).

### Is there a fee?

No. Registration is free.

### How do we register?

Use the [official website](https://apu-cloud-innovation-hackathon.gdgocapu.com/portal/). Each participant signs in with their own account and adds their details. The team leader creates the team and shares its private invite link with members.

### Need to correct a registration?

Ask in [#help-desk](help-desk.md) before submitting another registration, so organizers can help avoid duplicate team entries.

## Challenges and schedule

### Which problem statements can we choose from?

Smart Campus & Student Experience; Intelligent Automation & Digital Transformation; or Data-Driven Research & Intelligent Decision Making. Read [#problem-statements](problem-statements.md) for the descriptions and example ideas.

### Do we have to use an example idea?

No. The examples are starting points. Your project should address the challenge area your team selects.

### Does my Discord challenge tag register our choice?

No. The team leader selects your team's challenge in the registration workspace. The Discord tag only helps members find relevant conversations and can be changed later.

### When is the briefing?

The problem statement briefing and Gemini training are on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, 6:00–9:00 PM MYT, online via Bevy. The RSVP and joining link will be posted in [#announcements](announcements.md).

### When does registration close?

Registration closes Sunday, 8 November 2026 at 11:59 PM MYT. Check [#key-dates-and-briefing](key-dates-and-briefing.md) for the latest schedule.

### Where does the hackathon take place?

The build phase is online. Top 5 final pitching is currently planned for Tuesday, 17 November 2026 at APU Auditorium during NexConnect '26.

## Building, submissions, and help

### Are specific tools or a coding background required?

The theme is "When Cloud, IoT, and Network Converge." The challenge tracks also include AI and data-driven ideas. No required tech stack or experience level is listed on the website. Watch [#announcements](announcements.md) for confirmed rules.

### What do teams submit?

The team leader submits a working prototype with a project title, chosen challenge, summary of at least 50 characters, HTTPS repository link, and PDF pitch deck up to 10 MB. A demo link is optional. The deadline is 10 November 2026 at 11:59 PM MYT. See [#submission-guidelines](submission-guidelines.md).

Use the [team workspace](https://apu-cloud-innovation-hackathon.gdgocapu.com/portal/) to save a draft, review the entry, and confirm the final submission. Please do not send project files in public channels.

### Can we change team members or our problem statement after registering?

Please ask an organizer in [#help-desk](help-desk.md) before making changes. The process has not been announced yet.

### What are the cash prizes?

The live website lists RM4,000 in cash prizes: 1st RM2,000; 2nd RM1,000; 3rd RM700; 4th RM150; 5th RM150.

### Where should I ask a question?

Start with this FAQ, [#key-dates-and-briefing](key-dates-and-briefing.md), and [#resources](resources.md). If your answer is not there, ask in [#help-desk](help-desk.md).

### Where are official updates?

Follow [#announcements](announcements.md) and [GDGoC APU on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/gdgoc.apu/).

Please keep personal contact details, resumes, and form responses out of public channels.
