## Asking for help

Use this channel for questions about eligibility, teams, the briefing, problem statements, registration, or submissions. Please describe the issue without sharing private information such as phone numbers, email addresses, resumes, or form responses.

For confirmed dates and instructions, check [#announcements](announcements.md) and [#key-dates-and-briefing](key-dates-and-briefing.md) first. An organizer will respond when available.

## Support tickets

A ticket bot in the channel offers a **Create ticket** button that opens a private support ticket with the organizers.
