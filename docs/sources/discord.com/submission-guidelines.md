## Deadline

Tuesday, 10 November 2026 at 11:59 PM MYT.

## What to prepare

Prepare a working prototype and the following for your team entry:

- Project title and one selected challenge area
- Project summary of at least 50 characters
- HTTPS link to your code repository
- PDF pitch deck (maximum 10 MB)
- Demo link

## Final submission

The team leader manages the entry and confirms the final submission in the [team workspace](https://apu-cloud-innovation-hackathon.gdgocapu.com/portal/). Saving a draft is not a final submission; once submitted, the project and PDF are locked.

Please keep private team files and invite links out of public channels. Ask submission questions in [#help-desk](help-desk.md).
