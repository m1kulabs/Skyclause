# Astra idea ranking

**Investigate Recheck first:** help APU IT confirm that a reported Wi-Fi problem is usable again from the affected place before accepting closure. Its strongest advantage is a short path to real observations and a measurable helpdesk outcome. Its biggest unknown is whether APU actually needs a better recovery-verification process. **LastLink is the second choice** if a transport liaison can use measured service-gap evidence. These are research priorities, not validated products or a commitment to build five apps.

## Scoring matrix

The [brief's judging section](/docs/BRIEF.md#judging-and-prizes) gives five spoken dimensions and **no official weights or numerical rubric**. The following is our decision model. It must be replaced if official criteria appear.

| Dimension               | Weight | What earns a higher estimate                                                     | Evidence needed before claiming success                                                              |
| ----------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I — Impact              | 25%    | A consequential, repeated task for one named role                                | Observed volume × measured time saved, plus task completion and failure rates                        |
| T — Technical build     | 20%    | Meaningful 1P and 3P connector jobs, traceable fresh input and durable action    | Actual live read → Gemini Enterprise decision → approved write; named modes, timestamps and failures |
| R — Research            | 20%    | Specific firsthand pain, local relevance, contradictions and testable hypotheses | 6–8 interviews in two rounds, one timed observation, consented notes and an evidence-to-change trail |
| H — Time to impact      | 15%    | Short route to a useful pilot and credible three/six-month ownership             | Account access, deployment boundary, named willing owner and dated milestones                        |
| W — Day-to-day workflow | 20%    | A recurring trigger, assigned review, recovery checks and exception handling     | Multiple days of runs including failures, approval delay, repeat incidents and credential refresh    |

Scale: **1** has major unsupported dependencies; **2** has a plausible mechanism with substantial gaps; **3** has a credible partial path; **4** has a strong proposed fit supported by relevant evidence; **5** has especially direct structural fit to that dimension. A 5 is still a hypothesis, not an achieved result. Recheck's W=5 reflects a naturally repeating report/retest/review cycle, not demonstrated run history. Formula: `total = sum(weight × score) / 5`.

Feasibility, theme integration, differentiation, adoption friction, privacy and demo clarity are assessed separately below and in each brief. They do not earn additional numerical bonuses that double-count impact or time to impact.

## Hard gates and uncertainties

**Every candidate remains conditional.** Desktop research can establish a plausible data path but cannot prove an owner will adopt it or the sponsored account will work.

| Gate                        | Required proof by 4 November                                                                             | Current state / consequence                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Core platform and accounts  | Gemini Enterprise opens; live 1P read and reviewed 3P action work; reviewer-access path confirmed        | Unverified for all. Consumer Gmail alone is insufficient for the proposed Workspace route.                                                                     |
| Real data and rights        | One actual relevant observation with provenance; lawful handling and publication permission where needed | Team phones are available in principle, not measured here. Public-feed availability is not APU shuttle telemetry. LabRelay needs a physical temperature probe. |
| Physical signal and network | The signal changes the decision; a failed network/source changes the visible state                       | Strongest natural fit: Recheck and LabRelay. AccessRelay's camera/IMU and WashWitness's vibration need explicit limited claims.                                |
| Actionable owner            | One office contact agrees to review the output and names a useful next action                            | None secured. “IT helpdesk” is a proposed role, not a partner.                                                                                                 |
| Distinct value              | Existing app, ticket rule or spreadsheet cannot achieve equivalent value with less work                  | All five face credible substitutes; see each falsifier. No claim of global uniqueness.                                                                         |
| Reviewable prototype        | Real event → fresh read → inspectable decision → approval → real record, repeatable by a reviewer        | Not built. A team-owned Jira project demonstrates capability, not APU integration.                                                                             |

Failure of the first gate pauses the proposed architecture; do not quietly substitute a standalone chatbot and call it compliant. First verify another supported account-compatible 1P/3P path described in [connector research](/docs/research/gemini-enterprise-data.md); otherwise escalate the licence issue to organisers through the team. Custom MCP is optional and cannot rescue an unconfirmed organisation policy.

The scenarios use the same five dimension scores. They test preferences, not probabilities of winning.

| ----------- | ------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Recheck     | 84                  | 84                   | 84                          | 86                            |
| LastLink    | 77                  | 76                   | 77                          | 74                            |
| AccessRelay | 73                  | 72                   | 74                          | 72                            |
| WashWitness | 71                  | 72                   | 70                          | 75                            |
| LabRelay    | 70                  | 68                   | 71                          | 66                            |

WashWitness becomes second under delivery-first weights; AccessRelay and WashWitness tie under equal weights; LabRelay overtakes WashWitness under impact-first weights but still has the hardest access gate. Recheck leads these weight scenarios, **but not all plausible evidence scenarios**: if interviews lower its research score from 4→2 and daily-workflow score from 5→4, it falls to 72 and LastLink leads at 77. If LastLink's accessible evidence and rollout scores fall from 4→2 and 3→2, it falls to 66. Do not treat a 2–4 point difference as decisive.

## What changes across rounds

[The round log](06-ideation-rounds.md) contains twelve starting mechanisms, eliminations, three complete five-dimension scorecards for each finalist, and the concrete revisions behind score changes. The largest changes came from existing substitutes, mismatched public versus private transport data, approval latency, and lack of local evidence. The final shortlist centres on verified outcomes and accountable handoffs; a new dashboard alone does not clear the differentiation gate.

## Shared delivery contract

The product briefs inherit these constraints from [CONTEXT](/docs/CONTEXT.md#decisions-so-far) and [current feasibility research](/docs/research/astra-feasibility-and-substitutes.md):

- **Architecture:** physical observation → HTTPS feeder → current Sheet in a supported Workspace domain → live Sheets action in Gemini Enterprise → reviewed Jira create. A stored reading is historical evidence with its original time, never a substitute for a failed live source. For current status, validate source/collector heartbeat and freshness at read time. A healthy Sheet alone does not prove a healthy sensor.
- **Connector honesty:** 1P Sheets supplies current observations and versioned team-authored rules; 3P Jira receives real cases generated from those observations. Prove the action inventory first. Any Jira read, update, comment or transition beyond verified create is a capability gate; a reviewer can manually link or transition the existing issue. Do not create duplicate incidents to conceal a missing update action.
- **Agent role:** Gemini Enterprise interprets messy reports, requests missing context, assembles evidence and explains an action using bounded rules. Matching IDs, freshness, thresholds and deduplication are deterministic. Compare the agent against a form + rules + template baseline; remove the AI interpretation step if it adds errors without saving review effort.
- **Cadence and quotas:** feeder sampling is separate from model runs. Start one 30-minute batch workflow (48 runs/day) or a slower use-case cadence. A budget scenario of two metered queries/run plus 24 for review equals 120/day against 160 on one Standard seat; this is arithmetic, not measured billing. Measure actual usage over ten runs and slow or simplify if projected use exceeds 80% of the allocated pool. Show quota errors; do not silently retry indefinitely.
- **Approvals and operations:** a named team operator checks pending actions during stated pilot hours; publish that coverage. Outside it, cases remain visibly pending. Do not promise unattended resolution or emergencies. Refresh delegated credentials before 11 November, inspect Runs daily through 14 November, and retain exact action/approval times.
- **Sensor truth:** source time, observed time, ingest time and workflow time remain separate. Unknown, stale, failed, recovered and approved are distinct. A deliberately disconnected team device is a real controlled network test, labelled as such; it does not prove a campus outage. Never manipulate campus infrastructure to generate an incident.
- **Privacy and rights:** use team-owned measurements and real, minimal event records. Do not collect identities, diagnoses, credentials, MAC addresses, private timetables or account screenshots. Publish sanitized team sensor logs with provenance only with consent. Historical evidence and separately selected replay are labelled; no recorded fallback when live access fails. Keep social posts as research citations, not production training data.
- **Demo:** show the live workflow and reviewed action, a real source failure becoming unknown, and recovery requiring fresh evidence. If no natural incident occurs, show healthy observations and explain the untested incident path; a published real log may be demonstrated separately as replay. Reviewers still need live app access and their own executable trigger.

## Selection and submission plan

On **4 November**, test Recheck's connector chain and interview two affected users plus one helpdesk contact. Ask for the last actual incident, the evidence requested, how closure was decided, and the work that remained. Observe their current process before showing a design. If there is no actionable closure gap, select LastLink only after a transport owner and relevant live observations exist. Otherwise reconsider the remaining candidates using their stated kill criteria; no automatic fallback to invented data.

On **5–6 November**, complete two rounds totalling 6–8 interviews and one timed observation; record refusals and contradictory feedback. Start real runs. On **7 November**, evaluate 30–50 real cases or observation windows with independent human labels, hold out the final day, report numerator/denominator and failure reasons. Repeated samples of the same incident are not independent cases. If fewer exist, report the actual sample and uncertainty; do not pad it.

On **8–9 November**, freeze scope and package the evidence. The README covers existing problem, solution, research, team collaboration and impact; include connector modes, an architecture diagram, observed versus projected results, three/six-month milestones and reviewer instructions. Produce the PDF deck (at most 10 MB) and a labelled 3–4-minute recording from real operation. Use Antigravity for the feeder or integration work and document what it actually contributed.

On **10 November**, the leader checks the title, exactly one track, summary of at least 50 characters, HTTPS repository URL, PDF and demo link before final submission locks the entry. Keep live access and run evidence available for **11–14 November**. These are proposed build/submission tasks, not work already completed by this ideation exercise.
