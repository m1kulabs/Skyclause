## S09
Under the hood, it runs on Google Cloud.
Cloud Scheduler wakes our source adapters on Cloud Run.
Publish-subscribe and Cloud Tasks carry every reading with its age to the clause engine.
Gemini on Vertex AI drafts, a person approves,
and the record is sealed in Postgres with its keys in Cloud KMS.
Behind all of it, more than 4,400 automated tests pass,
many of them on real captures of the public feeds.
