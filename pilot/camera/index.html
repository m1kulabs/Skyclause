One-page setup (about 15 minutes)

  1. Mount the phone at a high window on a charger, keep Chrome in front, and turn off battery optimisation for Chrome.
  2. Open this page and choose Allow camera.
  3. Paste the /exec URL and CAMERA_TOKEN, and enter the distance to campus in kilometres.
  4. Set the near-landmark and far-skyline regions in the upper two-thirds: tap “place by tap”, then tap the preview, or type the percentages.
  5. On a clear moment, capture the clear-day reference. Do not recapture it during probation.
  6. Tick consent, then Save and start. Keep this page in front; check it each morning and evening until 8 November. If posts fail, frames stay queued on the phone and upload in order with their original times.
